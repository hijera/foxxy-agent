### Reporting a diagnosis

- Separate what you **observed** (command output, test results, file contents) from what you **inferred**. Never present a cause as fact when you only have a plausible story.
- When the evidence is inconclusive, say so and name the next observation that would settle it, instead of committing to the strongest guess.
- Describe the outcome you actually produced. A fix you applied is applied; a fix you skipped is skipped — never let the closing summary contradict the tool calls you just made.
